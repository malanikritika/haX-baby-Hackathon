# Pip's Dream Job 🌙☁️

A cozy, interactive website about **Pip**, a sleepy little cloud who works as a junior Dream Engineer at the Midnight Nursery. Visitors meet Pip, switch the world between night and day, and play through his dream-making projects.

**Live website:** https://malanikritika.github.io/haX-baby-Hackathon/

## About the project

Instead of describing a dream job on a page, we gave the job to a character and let visitors watch him do it. Pip floats, blinks, talks and tells his story. Then he leads you into the Dreamland Arcade and his other little worlds.

## Features

- **Meet Pip:** he floats, blinks, talks in a speech bubble and tells his story. Tap "Hear Pip again" to replay it.
- **Night and day mode:** one button changes the sky, colours, sun and moon.
- **Dreamland Arcade:** Lantern Lights Out and Starcatcher Rush, with levels, scores, timers and lives.
- **Nightlight Collection:** tap glowing lamps and lanterns that Pip designed.
- **Dream Delivery Routes:** plan Pip's rooftop route across town.
- **The Lullaby Lab:** wind the music box and hear Pip hum a new lullaby.
- **Pip's Secret Lullaby:** find the star that blinks differently, open three doors and tap the egg to hatch Pip.
- **Sound:** soft background music and music that plays as you move the cursor, each with its own mute and unmute button.
- **Responsive:** works on phones, tablets and laptops.

## Built with

- HTML5, CSS3 and vanilla JavaScript
- Canvas API for sparkles, stars and particles
- Web Audio API for music and sound effects
- Web Speech API for character voices
- Google Fonts (Fredoka and Nunito)

## How to run

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the folder in VS Code.
3. Right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with Live Server**, or just open `index.html` in your browser.

No installation or login is needed. Press a start button in a game first, because browsers block sound until you click.
